# Lab charter

## Purpose
ToxicTechLabs-RD exists to develop ambitious but disciplined research programs across robotics, chemistry, AI, and high-risk concept engineering.

## Boundaries
- Every project begins with a clear scientific question and measurable objective.
- All systems must be reviewed for safety, scope, and operational impact.
- Internal logs and financial records are maintained with accountability and traceability.
- No project is treated as final until benchmarked, reviewed, and documented.
