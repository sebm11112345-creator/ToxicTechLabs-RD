# ToxicTechLabs-RD

## Mission
ToxicTechLabs-RD is a research and development program focused on advanced automation, AI evaluation, speculative materials, and next-generation systems engineering. The lab combines rapid prototyping, high-contrast experiments, and operational discipline.

## Core domains
- Autonomous robotics
- AI testing and model evaluation
- Advanced materials discovery
- Speculative physics and field models
- Systems operations and financial tracking

## Operating thesis
